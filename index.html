About Us

We wish to spread smiles. At Dserve, we strongly believe that every individual is born with an innate talent. We strive towards empowering untapped talents from the underprivileged and persons with disability.

Our Initiatives

Two dedicated initiatives through which we work towards our mission.

Enable Arts

Showcasing the artistic talents of people with disability by promoting their work through various platforms, connecting them with mentors and helping them achieve financial independence.

Paatshala

Guiding underprivileged children in shaping their careers — identifying their calibre, fostering skills, and improving soft skills and communication.

Explore Our Initiatives

Empowering Tales

We curate narratives from both perspectives — individuals with disabilities and the resilient parents who faced challenges while raising their children — to inspire, inform, and foster societal awareness through storytelling.
