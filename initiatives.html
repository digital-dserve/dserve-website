Our Initiatives

Two dedicated initiatives through which we work towards our mission of empowering the underprivileged and disabled communities.

Enable Arts

Every individual is born with an innate talent. Many times, these talents go unnoticed and are not pursued further. The challenge lies in identifying and channelising it to achieve success.

"Enable Arts", a dedicated initiative of Dserve, aims to serve this purpose by discovering untapped talents from the disabled community and provide support in nurturing them. Our aim is to showcase the artistic talents of the disabled community by promoting their work through various platforms. Our vision is to act as a bridge to connect the talents with the right people and help them to achieve financial independence.

Paatshala

We strongly believe that shaping the youth of today is of paramount importance as they are the leaders of tomorrow. Although textbook learning can prepare a child for securing good grades, soft skills have become a prerequisite for shining beyond the walls of a classroom.

For fulfilling this mission, we at Dserve have launched a dedicated initiative named "Paatshala". We intend to accomplish this by organising various workshops on communication and soft skill development, thereby laying out the path towards a promising career.

Want to be a part of this?

Whether you have a skill to share or time to give, there's a place for you at Dserve.
